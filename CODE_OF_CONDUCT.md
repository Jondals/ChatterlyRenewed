# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a friendly place for everyone.

- Be respectful. No insults, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Assume good intentions and explain your point of view calmly.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Do not publish other people's private information.

People who break these rules may have their comments removed and may be blocked from the project. To report a problem, write to the maintainer through the contact in the repository profile.
